What is SEVOR?

A marketplace for temporary access.

SEVOR connects renters looking for something to use for a limited time with owners and providers who have suitable items available to rent.

Renters

People or organizations seeking access to an item for a defined period.

Owners & providers

Individuals or companies making suitable items available through listings.

Rental opportunities

A place to discover, review, request, and complete rentals when listings are available.

Why buy something you may only need for a short time?

A tool, camera, vehicle, device, or other item may be useful for a day, a week, a project, or an occasion. Ownership can make sense in some situations; in others, temporary access may be worth exploring.

  • Discover rental options when they are available.
  • Help suitable unused items become useful to someone else.

A practical choice

Rent and buy solve different needs.

BUY

Choose ownership

  • Pay the full purchase price.
  • Keep the item.
  • Store and maintain it.
  • Useful when ownership makes sense.
RENT

Choose temporary access

  • Pay for agreed temporary access.
  • Use the item during the rental period.
  • Return it afterward.
  • Useful when temporary access makes more sense.

For companies

Company accounts can take part in the same rental marketplace.

SEVOR currently recognizes individual and company account types. A company can use the existing listing and booking flow for suitable rental needs or offers; this page does not imply separate enterprise tools.

A company can be an owner, a renter, or both—depending on the available listings and its needs.

Suitable equipment or assets can be made available through listings. A company that needs temporary access can also explore the same marketplace. The platform’s booking model is shared rather than a separate business-only flow.

  • Company registration includes a company-proof step.
  • Explore can filter listings from companies or individuals.

Built around practical trust tools

Tools that support informed rental decisions.

SEVOR includes mechanisms that help users document, communicate about, and manage rentals. They support the process; they are not a guarantee of any outcome.

Identity & company verification

The platform collects verification documents, including ID card, driver’s licence, passport, and company proof, with review paths.

Booking records

Booking requests, decisions, and rental-related steps are recorded within the current flow.

Rental communication

Item-linked messaging helps eligible participants communicate about a rental.

Evidence paths

Pickup, return, and deposit-related evidence tools exist where the rental flow requires them.

Reviews & ratings

Booking-linked feedback helps users learn from previous rental experiences without guaranteeing quality.

Support & reports

Support tickets and listing-report paths are available through the existing platform tools.

Accepted online bookings use the current PayPal payment flow.

Owners can maintain payout preferences for Interac, PayPal, or Wise in the current account settings. The current payout workflow is manual, so this page makes no promise about timing, availability, or a payout result.

  • A security amount can apply to some bookings.
  • Deposit evidence and review paths apply where relevant.
