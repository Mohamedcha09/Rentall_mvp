SEVOR

Introduction

SEVOR operates a website and mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how SEVOR collects, uses, and protects information connected to the platform.

Information we collect

The current published policy identifies the following categories of information.

Account information

Name, email address, and phone number.

Booking & rental information

Information connected to bookings and rentals.

Uploaded photos

Photos uploaded for verification, pickup, and return.

Device & usage information

Device information and usage data.

How we use information

SEVOR's current policy lists these purposes for using information.

  • Provide and improve rental services.
  • Verify identity.
  • Process bookings and payments.
  • Enhance safety and help prevent fraud.
  • Communicate about an account.

Your privacy rights

The current policy states that you may request the following actions regarding your personal data.

Access your data

Request access to your personal data.

Correct information

Request correction of your personal data.

Request deletion

Request deletion of your personal data.

Account & data deletion

Use the existing account-deletion flow

SEVOR has an existing account-deletion page for signed-in users. It requires sign-in and uses the platform's current deletion flow; this page does not promise the scope or timing of deletion beyond that flow.

Open account deletion Open support

Privacy & security

The published policy identifies identity verification, booking and payment processing, safety, and fraud prevention as reasons for using information. It does not publish a specific technical security standard, so this page makes no such claim.
